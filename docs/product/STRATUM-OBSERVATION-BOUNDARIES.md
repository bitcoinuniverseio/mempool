# Stratum V2 observation boundaries

The observatory renders role, template and job-declaration responses, including rejected declarations, rejection codes, miner-declared and pool-modified transaction lists and latency. Plaintext/degraded roles and superseded templates do not receive successful-security styling. Selected-network changes immediately clear old telemetry and cancel its requests.

All three owned telemetry sources remain unavailable in this deployment. UI contract and cancellation tests do not establish live SV2 role operation, pool acceptance or miner transaction-selection authority. Those original operations remain open until actual owned telemetry and corresponding user journeys are verified.
