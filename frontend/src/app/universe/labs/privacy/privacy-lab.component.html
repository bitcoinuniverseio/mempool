What this page will not do

  • It does not name anyone, infer an owner, or attach a label to an address. It reads a transaction's structure, and structure is all it reports.
  • It has no risk score, no watch list, and no notion of a transaction being suspicious. Those are judgements about people, and this reads transactions.
  • It uses no outside source. Every rule reads the one transaction in front of it, from this node or from your own hexadecimal.
  • It stores nothing. What you paste is discarded when you leave, and no record of what you looked at is kept anywhere.
  • It does not pretend to certainty. Every finding says what would make it wrong, in both directions, next to the finding itself.
