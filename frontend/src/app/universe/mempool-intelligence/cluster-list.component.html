Mempool clusters

A cluster is a group of unconfirmed transactions tied together by spending, so a miner has to take them in order. This is what a miner chooses from, rather than a flat list of transactions.

Mempool packages

Only the clusters that hold a real dependency: an unconfirmed transaction spending another unconfirmed transaction. These are the cases where a miner cannot take one without the other.

Why clusters and not transactions

A transaction that spends an unconfirmed output cannot be mined without its parent. So a cheap parent with a rich child is worth more together than the parent looks alone, and a rich parent with a cheap child does not become cheaper. Ordering by each transaction's own fee rate produces a block no miner could build.
