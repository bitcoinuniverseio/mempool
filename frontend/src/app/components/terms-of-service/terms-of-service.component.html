What this is

Universe Explorer is a free, read-only view of the Bitcoin network and of the Bitcoin Universe protocols indexed by Bitcoin Universe's own infrastructure. It is provided as it is, with no warranty of any kind and no guarantee of availability.

Accuracy

Base chain data comes from a Bitcoin node operated by Bitcoin Universe. Protocol asset data comes from first-party indexers, and every claim on a page states the authority and the block it was proven at. Where an authority cannot prove something, the page says so instead of guessing. Nothing here is financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

What it will not tell you

The explorer describes what transactions do on chain. It does not describe intent. It will not tell you that something was bought, sold, or traded, because a transaction alone does not prove that.

Fair use of the API

The public API is shared with everyone. Please keep request rates reasonable, cache what you can, and do not scrape the site in ways that degrade it for other people. Excessive traffic may be rate limited or blocked.

No advertising

This explorer carries no advertising and no sponsored placement. Rankings and trend figures are computed from published rules and cannot be bought.
