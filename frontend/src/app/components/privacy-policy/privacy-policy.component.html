Privacy

Last updated 26 August 2026.

What this explorer collects

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no profile. The explorer sets no advertising or analytics cookies and loads no third-party tracker, script, or font.

Searches and browsing

Whatever you type into the search box is matched in your browser first. The explorer only asks its own backend for the specific transaction, block, address, output, or asset you opened, and it never sends your query anywhere else.

What your browser stores

Recently viewed items, saved pages, pinned protocols, and display preferences are kept in your own browser's local storage. They are never sent to the server and never leave your device. You can see and erase all of it on the saved page.

Server logs

The web server keeps short-lived operational logs so faults can be diagnosed and abuse can be rate limited. They are not used to build a profile of any visitor and are not shared.

Where the data comes from

Every figure on this site is read from Bitcoin Universe's own Bitcoin node, Electrum index, and protocol authorities. No third-party blockchain API, public explorer, or hosted indexer is called at any point, from the server or from your browser.

Run it yourself

A trusted third party is still a third party. The complete source of this explorer is published, so you can verify what it does and host it yourself. See Source and licenses.
